What changed — and what did not

Both zombies independently minimize Manhattan distance to the old human position. They may overlap; there is no blocking or coordination. Human greedy maximizes distance to the nearest old zombie. All agents choose simultaneously from the old state. Shared-cell or cardinal contact with either zombie captures before or after a move, with pairwise crossing compatibility. Capture takes priority over recurrence, then the 10,000-tick safety cutoff.

Both planners use the exact known zombie model and the same score: completed noncapturing transitions first, then nearest-zombie endpoint distance. They enumerate legal north, east, south, west, stay sequences depth-first; exact ties keep the first. Captured branches stop immediately. Only the first move is executed, then they replan. Hypothetical search uses a copied clock, independent of real remaining time.

One-tick versus two-tick isolates added look-ahead depth. Greedy versus either planner changes both prediction and scoring, not depth alone. No memory, randomness, learning, training or general-intelligence claim.
