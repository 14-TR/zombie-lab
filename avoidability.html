What the distinction means

Avoidable policy failure

A tested policy is caught, but some legal strategy avoids capture indefinitely. A safe repeating witness demonstrates that possibility. Longer survival alone is not indefinite avoidance.

Unavoidable capture & maximum delay

Every legal strategy is caught. A positive certificate rank is the maximum number of ticks the human can delay capture, not an average or a planning horizon. Rank 0 means initial contact, before any policy can act.

The graph covers all 343,000 ordered H/Z1/Z2 states, including overlap and contact. Terminal states have rank 0. A losing state has rank 1 + the maximum successor rank only when every legal successor is losing. Ranks strictly decrease under every action, ruling out even history-dependent escapes. Remaining winning states each have a safe winning successor.

What is verified here

Every selected stored frame is checked against legal human actions and the frozen ZombiePair.resolveTick contact/crossing rules. Both zombies independently choose their first minimum-distance N/E/S/W/stay move from the same old human position. Capture takes priority over ordered-position recurrence. The exact outcome, stop tick, cycle start and period must match; there is no simulation cutoff in this witness validation.

The three selected legacy trajectories are also recomputed and compared with their frozen outcome and recurrence fields. Their first one-tick/two-tick action divergence is identified from actual moves, not outcome labels. Any mismatch clears the replay, counts and controls.

A witness validates a legal trajectory, not all graph ranks. The full certificate and independent checker establish the universal losing-rank and winning-set claims; this browser does not recheck the whole graph or authenticate source hashes. Metadata records provenance. No adversarial zombies, randomness, training, learning or real-world intelligence claim.
