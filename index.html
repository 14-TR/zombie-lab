H · Human / circle flees. Z · Zombie / square pursues.
Both choose from the same old positions, then move together.

10 × 7 bounded grid. Move one cell north, east, south, west, or stay. The human maximizes Manhattan distance; the zombie minimizes it. Ties: north → east → south → west → stay. Coordinates start at (0, 0) in the top-left; x increases right, y down.

A shared cell or orthogonally adjacent cells (Manhattan distance 1, not diagonal) means caught. Check before movement (including tick 0) and after simultaneous moves; exchanged positions still count. Capture takes priority over the tick limit. Stop on capture or at tick 40. Reset restores H (7, 2), Z (2, 4).
