What does sensitivity mean?

At each permitted human cell, sensitivity is the maximum absolute difference in stopTick from its permitted north, east, south and west human-start neighbors, excluding the zombie cell and out-of-board cells. Diagonals do not count. Ties choose the lowest neighbor ID; an empty neighbor set is shown as “—”, not zero.

This is a local timing difference, not a change in capture outcome. The orange inset marks the inspected sensitivity cell.

Scope and provenance

Only starting positions change. Both agents choose simultaneous cardinal/stay moves using Manhattan distance and the unchanged production tie-break order north, east, south, west, stay. Capture is shared-cell or orthogonal adjacency (including tick 0), with crossing compatibility. The runner checks capture, first repeated ordered position pair, then the safety limit.

Maps use frozen scalar results; only the selected A/B trajectories are recomputed. The replay checks outcome, stopping tick, cycle start and period against those rows. Endpoint coordinates are recomputed, not stored in the frozen rows. The linked certificate is optional supporting evidence: this page does not load or verify it and does not depend on its availability.
