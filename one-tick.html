Exported evidence and limitations

Both selected trajectories use the frozen model and production resolveTick with old-state greedy zombie moves. Every outcome, stop tick, cycle start and period must match; every supplied witness frame is compared. A mismatch clears playback and downloads. Other starts are indexed, not replayed at page load. This browser does not independently authenticate source hashes, the exact-rank certificate, training provenance or held-out aggregate counts. No new training or model selection is performed.
