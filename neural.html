Verification & provenance

For the selected start, every human action is recomputed by NeuralPolicy.chooseHuman with the immutable model, then passed to production ZombiePair.resolveTick. Each zombie independently chooses its first nearest N/E/S/W/stay destination using the same old human position. No solver, safety shield, or search is available to neural inference.

Capture takes precedence over first ordered-position recurrence, then the 10,000-tick unresolved cutoff. Outcome, stopping tick, cycle start and period must match the frozen row; all supplied witness frames must also match. Other policy entries above are stored comparisons, not independently replayed here. Any mismatch clears stale results and disables replay.

Recorded hashes are provenance, not browser-authenticated source identity. This selected replay does not recheck the full exact-rank certificate, train/test membership digest, model training, or all evaluation trajectories.
