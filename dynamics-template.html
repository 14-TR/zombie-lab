Interpretation limits
Observations-only is conditional on the shared movement/capture scaffold and a four-law candidate family. Cases A/B identify the active law within that family; C/D retain three hypotheses. Neither condition retains all four. Correct abstention is expected when their futures differ. The exact ceiling uses privileged truth in hidden-rule conditions.
All frozen cases are uncaught at tick 1 and caught by tick 3; capture classification is not balanced within a horizon. The 12×9 geometry is new; “unchanged” refers to the historical transition law, not the board dimensions. New starts lie outside the prior 10×7 coordinate support. Paired results are descriptive only; request order is fixed, and shared configurations/horizons are not independent samples.
No action selection, safety mask, model-driven trajectory, retries, warmups, installation or publication. Independent transition algorithms agree; independent implementation review is a separate parent-owned gate.
