# ZL-011 — Tiny feed-forward imitation (preregistered)

Base b22cf4990f10a694998e4a571b534ece8d46cd43. Question: can one tiny learned positional policy reduce the two planners' avoidable captures without new failures? Negative results complete the experiment; no architecture/hyperparameter tuning after seeing evaluation. No GRU/RL or claim of independent discovery.

## Frozen training
- Use all nonterminal ordered states of ZL010's343000-state graph for labeled examples, excluding held-out groups below. Targets from unchanged exact ranks/production transitions: uniform probability over every legal successor with rank -1 for winning states; uniform over all maximum-rank successors for losing states. No training targets from test/validation examples.
- Group by unordered zombie cell pair (a=min(z1,z2),b=max(z1,z2)) so swapping identical pursuer roles cannot cross splits. SHA256 UTF8 `ZL011:a:b`; first8 hex digits as unsigned integer modulo10:0..7 train,8 validation,9 test. All human positions in a group share split. Freeze counts/membership digest before training. This holds out configurations, NOT entire trajectories; test trajectories may later visit training-group states. Disclose that limitation and measure visited-group overlap. No symmetry/data augmentation.
- Inputs [hx/9,hy/6,z1x/9,z1y/6,z2x/9,z2y/6]. One hidden32-unit tanh layer,5 linear logits.389 scalar trainable parameters. Float64 training and JS inference. W1 shape6×32 row-major, b1 length32, W2 shape32×5 row-major,b2 length5. Softmax over legal moves only. Illegal moves masked; no solver/shield/search at inference. First maximum logit in N/E/S/W/stay order chooses destination. Initial contact returns terminal before decision.
- NumPy already available; no installation. One run seed17 using numpy.random.default_rng(17), Xavier uniform weights sqrt(6/(fanIn+fanOut)), zero biases.40 epochs, batch512 including final short batch; fresh RNG permutation each epoch. Adam lr0.003,beta1=.9,beta2=.999,epsilon1e-8, batch-mean soft-target cross-entropy. Standard bias correction, no weight decay/clipping. Final epoch40 model only, no validation checkpoint choice. Numerical failure or training walltime600s stops honestly, not a silently shortened successful run. Limit BLAS threads1. Test gradients with central finite differences before real training.
- Export initial untrained and final models and epoch train/validation losses; evaluation test labels are inaccessible to trainer. Record actual versions, steps, seed, source/data/weights hashes, walltime/RSS/storage. Keep training and decision latency costs separate.
