What the planner knows — and what changed

The planner explicitly predicts the known zombie dynamics. It enumerates legal two-action human sequences in north, east, south, west, stay order. At each hypothetical transition the zombie acts on the old state, never the candidate new human position. The score first maximizes completed noncapturing transitions (0–2), then endpoint Manhattan distance; exact ties keep the first sequence. Only the first human action is executed, then the planner replans.

Hypothetical search ignores the real clock cutoff; real execution uses the unchanged simultaneous resolver and actual clock. Shared-cell or cardinal adjacency captures (including tick 0), with crossing compatibility. Capture takes priority over first repetition, then safety cutoff. No memory, randomness, learning or training. This tests this combined model-and-scoring policy, not isolated look-ahead depth or general intelligence.

Maps index frozen scalar rows; only the selected pair is replayed through the actual baseline/planner step functions. Outcome, stop tick, cycle start and period must match those rows or playback is cleared and disabled. Endpoint coordinates come from production replay; they are not stored in the scalar artifact.
