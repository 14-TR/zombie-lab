# ZL-014 — Jev predictive world-model pilot (preregistered)

Baseline: `7bb12e5b0c84bd2f14c2e90360dfca64c8313d17`. World, legacy policies, neural models and historical evidence are unchanged. This is a prediction pilot, with action choice evaluated separately, not a closed-loop policy or learned planner. No training, installs, GPU, service or API endpoint in the public viewer.

## Frozen selection and questions

Enumerate the original fixed-Z1 slice (10×7 board, Z1 cell 42), ascending ordered state ID `((z1*70+z2)*70+h)`, cell `y*10+x`. Exclude illegal/overlapping human starts and all initial contacts (Manhattan distance ≤1 to either zombie). From the resulting N IDs take index `floor(i*(N-1)/23)` for i=0..23, including both endpoints. This is a purposive finite pilot, neither exhaustive nor random/heldout evidence. All legal human actions for each selected state are included.

One request per state: two independent Choice questions for the zombies' next moves, one Noul for immediate capture per human action, one Noul for successor indefinite avoidability per human action, and a separate human-action Choice. At most 13 questions (hard limit 18). Human objective: prefer any action admitting indefinite avoidance, otherwise maximize capture delay; choose first tied action in N/E/S/W/stay order. Zombie objective: minimize Manhattan distance to the OLD human position, first tie N/E/S/W/stay. All moves are simultaneous, in bounds, overlap allowed for zombies. Capture is shared cell/cardinal adjacency before or after motion, or exchanged positions. Indefinite avoidance means existence of a future human strategy against these deterministic zombies, not absence of capture at a finite cutoff.

Requests contain only world rules, old positions, and legal candidates. No oracle answers, ranks, scores, recommended moves, demonstrations, or other question responses are sent. Exact prompt strings and all 24 request bytes will be frozen and committed before inference, together with selected states and paired oracle labels. Truth uses unchanged `two-zombies.js` and the original ZL-010 certificate; an independently structured checker validates every selected state/action and certificate truth before inference. Boundary/contact/tie tests are offline only.

## Frozen evaluation

Report both zombie move accuracies and joint accuracy. Binary classifications use p≥0.5; compute capture and successor-avoidability accuracy, Brier mean `(p-truth)^2`, truth class totals and coverage. Missing/invalid answers count incorrect in full-denominator accuracy; full-denominator Brier assigns missing/invalid a worst-case loss of 1, explicitly labeled a missingness-penalized score, alongside valid-answer-only Brier and its denominator. Invalid answers are distinct from model mistakes. A malformed question does not silently discard other valid questions; wrong model invalidates the whole response. Validate exact Choice option set, finite bounded probabilities summing to 1 within 1e-5, chosen maximum (ties allowed), confidence in [0,1]; Noul must be finite numeric [0,1].

Evaluate human Choice directly against oracle successors, never compose predicted risk into a policy. Report selected-action immediate safety, selected-action avoidability (all 24, plus oracle-winning-state subset), objective-optimal action and canonical agreement; unavailable choices count failures. Report impossible-to-avoid states separately. Include every paired oracle row and actual one-step successor. Descriptive performance only: 24 states do not establish calibration, general intelligence, learning, planning or closed-loop survival. No prompt tuning, sample replacement, thresholds fitted to outcomes, rationale generation or cherry-picked exclusions. Negative results are completed science.
